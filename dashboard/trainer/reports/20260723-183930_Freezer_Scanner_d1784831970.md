Mazda **noticed** this and recorded it honestly in `problems`, but **stored the incomplete amount
anyway** and did not re-run `check_duplicates` against the changed value. The judge's deterministic
rules don't check cross-parse amount consistency, so it passed a record that Mazda's own evidence
says is wrong. This is a wrapper gap, not a one-off model slip: "flag the discrepancy" was treated
as sufficient instead of "reconcile before storing."

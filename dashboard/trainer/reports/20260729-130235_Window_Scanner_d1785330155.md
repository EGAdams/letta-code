## Wrapper defect diagnosed

Mazda's own learned **Rule 13** (proposal 90, aggregate_duplicate, 2026-07-19) exists
precisely for this pattern: *"When receipt text indicates a summary or acknowledgment
of multiple prior payments, perform aggregate-duplicate review against vendor history
before any save step; do not blanket-store or rely only on exact date/amount duplicate
checks."* The classifier's own `reason` field said exactly that ("evidence of payments
already made"), but Mazda ran `check_duplicates` **only against the aggregate total**
(one call, $247.70 / 2025-12-22), never against the 10 individual real contribution
dates/amounts her own parser had just extracted. She then itemized the aggregate into
10 children without ever checking whether those historical donations were already
recorded (e.g. from earlier bank-statement processing to the same vendor). `judge_trace`
scored this PASS because it only checks deterministic mechanics (duplicate-check ran,
category resolved, store succeeded) — it cannot detect that the *scope* of the duplicate
check was wrong for this document type. This is exactly the class of failure the Trainer
exists to catch.

## Wrapper defect diagnosed

`judge_trace`'s deterministic checks do not grade the statement report-generation
contract (report existence, hydration, audit status) — all three traces (292, 294, 296)
returned PASS regardless of the report state. This is why a fabricated report and a
knowingly-false success callback both sailed through the automated judge; only Trainer
inspection caught them. Worth a future wrapper improvement: extend the deterministic
statement-branch judge to check `report_generated`/`report_audit_status` for internal
consistency (e.g. reject `report_audit_status="unknown"` or a PASS verdict when a
canonical report tool call is absent from the trace).

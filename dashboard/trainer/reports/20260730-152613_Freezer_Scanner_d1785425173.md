## Wrapper defect diagnosed (does not change the verdict)

`itemize_existing_expense` crashed with a raw MySQL `OperationalError
(chk_expense_amount_pos)` instead of returning a structured `itemizable:false` with a
reason, when handed a receipt whose "items" are actually a balance-forward summary
(`+Previous Balance`, `-Payment received`) rather than billed purchase lines. The
Trainer manual requires itemization only for genuine multi-line billed items; an
invoice/utility-bill balance recap is not that. Two candidate fixes, either sufficient
on its own:
1. `itemize_existing_expense` should validate that all `unit_price`/`line_total`
   values are non-negative *before* attempting the insert, and return
   `{"itemizable": false, "reason": "line item <desc> has non-positive amount"}`
   instead of letting the DB constraint raise an uncaught `OperationalError`.
2. Alternatively, the wrapper's itemization-trigger rule ("multiple line items →
   itemize") should exclude invoice/statement-style balance-forward line items
   (previous balance, payment received, finance charge) from the "billed line items"
   definition, so Mazda doesn't attempt itemization on this document class at all.

This is a **tool robustness gap**, not a Mazda misjudgment — she never called it with
bad data on purpose, and she correctly folded the failure into a fail-closed
`itemized:false` outcome in her own evidence. No corrective message was sent to Mazda
this run; nothing about her behavior needs to change. Recommend routing this to
`propose_improvement` territory only if it recurs on a future balance-forward document
and produces a worse outcome than "left standalone."
